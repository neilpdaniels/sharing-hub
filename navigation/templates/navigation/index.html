Why rentalution exists

Borrowing from your neighbours is better for your wallet and better for the planet — but it's always been too hard to organise. rentalution connects people who need things with people who own them, nearby.

Every rental is covered by our structured transaction process: deposit holds, condition verification and a clear dispute path — so both sides are always protected.

We charge a small transparent fee on completed rentals. Registration and listing are always free.

Common questions

Who am I renting from?

You rent directly from other users nearby. rentalution matches you and handles the transaction structure — we're not the owner or the borrower.

Is my deposit safe?

Deposits are held as card authorisation holds and are only released once both sides confirm the return. Nothing is charged unfairly.

What if something goes wrong?

Our structured returns process includes condition video evidence and a dispute path. Both lender and borrower are protected throughout.

What does rentalution charge?

Registration and listing are free. rentalution charges a small percentage fee only on completed rentals — full details are in Fees & Charges.

Transparent pricing

Free

Accounts are free. Listing items and placing rental enquiries are always free.

Platform fee

A small percentage fee is charged on completed rentals only. No charge if a rental doesn't happen.

Deposit hold

A card authorisation hold covers the deposit. Funds are never moved unless a dispute is resolved in the lender's favour.

No hidden fees

All fees are shown before you confirm. Lenders and borrowers both see a full cost breakdown at agreement.
