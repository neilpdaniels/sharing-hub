Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 May 2026

This policy explains how rentalution uses cookies and similar technologies in line with UK GDPR and PECR.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and remember preferences.

Cookie Categories

  • Strictly necessary cookies: required for core features such as login, security, and session handling.
  • Preference cookies: remember choices like interface or location settings.
  • Analytics/measurement cookies: help us understand usage and improve the service.

Your Choices

  • You can accept or reject non-essential cookies from the cookie banner.
  • Strictly necessary cookies remain active because the service cannot run properly without them.
  • You can also clear cookies from your browser settings at any time.

How Long Cookies Last

Some cookies are session-based and expire when your browser closes. Others are persistent and remain for a set period unless deleted.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
