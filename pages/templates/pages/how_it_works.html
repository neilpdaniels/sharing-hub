Deposits

We protect deposits with a card hold

Instead of taking money up front in every case, we place a hold on the borrower’s card for the deposit amount. That means the funds are reserved, but not usually taken unless there’s a valid reason to collect them.

  • The hold gives the lender confidence without making the process feel heavy.
  • The borrower keeps visibility of what’s reserved and when it’s released.
  • Condition evidence is captured at both collection and return so there’s a clear record if anything needs reviewing.
  • If there’s an issue, the deposit can be reviewed through the transaction workflow.
