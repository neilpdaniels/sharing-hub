Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 May 2026

This policy explains how rentalution collects, uses, and protects personal data in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who We Are

rentalution is the data controller for personal data processed through this platform.

What We Collect

  • Account data: name, username, email address, mobile number, date of birth.
  • Profile and listing data: profile image, item listings, messages, booking details.
  • Location data: postcode/town and optional device location when you choose to share it.
  • Technical data: device/browser information, IP address, and security logs.

Why We Use Your Data

  • To provide account access, listings, messaging, and transactions.
  • To protect users and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To support customer service and improve platform reliability.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

Lawful Bases

  • Contract: providing the services you request.
  • Legitimate interests: platform safety, security, and service improvement.
  • Legal obligation: compliance, accounting, and dispute handling.
  • Consent: non-essential cookies and optional features where required.

Sharing Your Data

We may share data with service providers that support hosting, messaging, notifications, and payment-related operations, and where required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for service delivery, legal obligations, and dispute resolution. Retention periods may vary by data type.

Your Rights

Subject to legal limits, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and data portability. You can also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Contact and Complaints

For privacy requests, contact support through the Help and Support page. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you are unhappy with how your data is handled.
