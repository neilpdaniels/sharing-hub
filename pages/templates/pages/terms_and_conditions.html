Terms and Conditions

These terms explain how rentalution works, what we expect from users, and how bookings, deposits, and support are handled.

1. Using rentalution

By using the site or app, you agree to act honestly, keep your account details accurate, and follow the rules that apply to each booking or listing.

You must not use rentalution for anything unlawful, misleading, abusive, or harmful to another user.

Bookings

A booking becomes a live transaction once both parties confirm the rental steps. Dates, messages, and evidence are then tracked through the booking workflow.

Deposits

Deposits are usually handled by placing a hold on the borrower’s card. Depending on the outcome of the booking, the hold may be released, returned, or reviewed.

Evidence

Condition evidence may be captured at collection and return so both sides have a clear record if anything needs review later.

2. User responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information when creating a listing, enquiry, or transaction.
  • Keep rental items in the condition described, apart from fair wear and tear.
  • Respond promptly to booking steps, messages, and evidence requests.
  • Return items on time and in the agreed condition.
  • Use the platform respectfully and keep communication professional.

3. Payments, fees, and holds

rentalution may charge fees for using the platform or for specific services, and those fees will be shown before you complete a relevant action.

Where a deposit hold is required, you authorise the platform or its payment partner to place and later release or collect that hold according to the booking outcome and the relevant transaction workflow.

4. Cancellations, disputes, and feedback

Some bookings can only be cancelled if both parties agree, or if the transaction reaches a stage where the booking rules allow it.

If there is a disagreement about handover, condition, or deposit return, the booking may move into mediation or dispute review. Both parties can also leave feedback at the end of the rental.

Feedback should be honest, fair, and based on the actual booking experience.

5. Contact

If you need help understanding these terms, or think something on the platform needs attention, please contact admin@rentalution.com.
