# Phone screenshots

Store the selected Google Play screenshots here in their upload order, for
example:

1. `01-browse-listings.png`
2. `02-listing-details.png`
3. `03-booking.png`
4. `04-messages.png`

Google Play requires 2–8 PNG/JPEG screenshots at 16:9 or 9:16, with every
side from 320 to 3,840 px and each file no larger than 8 MB. Four screenshots
with at least 1,080 px on each side are needed for promotion eligibility.
